# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Using this project to build cheats, or to harass or dox players. This tool is
  for server administration and analysis, not for targeting individuals.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainer privately (see `SECURITY.md` for a private contact channel).
Maintainers will review and respond, and may remove, edit, or reject
contributions, or ban contributors, for behavior they deem inappropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
